Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start tracking time?

Open the Today tab, enter a title for your task, and tap the play button. Your timer will keep running even if you close the app.

Can I pause a running timer?

Yes. Tap the pause button on an active timer. Paused time is excluded from your total duration automatically.

How do I plan tasks for the week?

Use the Planning tab to create tasks with estimated durations. You can set them as recurring (daily, weekly, etc.) and start timers directly from planned tasks.

What is Focus Mode?

Focus Mode lets you block distracting apps while your timer is running, helping you stay productive during work sessions.

How do I edit a time entry?

Go to the Entries tab, find the entry you want to change, and tap on it to open the edit sheet where you can modify the title, times, client, and tags.

Is my data stored locally?

Yes. All your data is stored on your device. Clockwise is offline-first and does not upload your time entries to any server.
