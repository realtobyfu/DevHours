Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 16, 2026

Overview

Clockwise ("the App") is developed by Tobias Fu. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the App collects and how it is used.

Data Collection

Clockwise is designed with privacy in mind. The App stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

The following data is created and stored locally on your device only:

  • Time entries (titles, start/end times, durations)
  • Planned tasks and schedules
  • Client names and project information
  • Tags and categories
  • App preferences and settings

Third-Party Services

Clockwise does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If you subscribe to Clockwise Premium, your purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple's privacy policy governs the handling of payment information. We do not have access to your payment details.

Focus Mode & Screen Time

If you enable Focus Mode, the App uses Apple's Screen Time framework to manage app restrictions on your device. This data stays on your device and is managed entirely by iOS. We do not have access to information about which apps you block.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties. Your time tracking data never leaves your device.

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device for as long as the App is installed. Deleting the App will remove all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

Clockwise does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The App does not collect personal data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact
